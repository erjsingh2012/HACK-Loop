[K] Knowledge: The timeout trap actively converted the silent SDK hangs into explicit, manageable error sinks (FB_SDK_TIMEOUT). This unblocked the client’s internal retry handlers, immediately restoring the dashboard metrics and revenue flow. Furthermore, the telemetry generated by the trap served as the definitive architectural evidence for the vendor escalation, leading to an automated platform sanity-check suite added to the technical backlog.
